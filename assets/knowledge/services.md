## How a build runs
1. **Intro call**: Thirty minutes, free, booked on Cal.com. Your goal, your constraints, and an honest answer on whether AI is even the right tool.
2. **Written scope**: Deliverables, milestones and timeline in writing, agreed before any work starts.
3. **Build in the open**: Weekly demos on a staging link, so you see progress every week instead of at the end.
4. **Handover**: Your repo, your docs, your evaluation suite. You own all the code, and the first 2 weeks of fixes after launch are included.

## Questions
**What hours can you take calls?** 2pm to 11pm Pakistan time, which covers UK and EU afternoons and American East Coast mornings. I reply to every message within 24 hours.

**Who owns the code?** You do. The repo, the docs and the evaluation suite are yours from the first commit.

**Will you sign an NDA?** Yes. Send it before the first call and I will sign it before any details are shared.

**What do you build with?** Python, FastAPI and LangGraph for agents and RAG; Vapi or Retell for voice; n8n or Make for automation; React, Next.js, Expo and Supabase for products. I pick what fits your stack, not the other way round.

**How do you know an AI build actually works?** Every AI build ships with an evaluation suite that runs in CI, so a change that makes answers worse fails before it reaches your users. A full OWASP red-team audit is available as an add-on.

**What happens after launch?** Two weeks of fixes are included. After that, ongoing support is available if you want it.

**What do you need from me to start?** A thirty-minute call about the problem. If there is a fit, I send a written scope before any work begins.
