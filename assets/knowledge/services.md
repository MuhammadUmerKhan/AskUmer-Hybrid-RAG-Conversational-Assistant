## AI voice agents
An AI voice agent answers or places phone calls, holds a natural conversation and then acts on it: it checks your calendar, books the slot and writes the lead into your CRM. I build them on Vapi and Retell AI, and on a custom real-time stack when a product needs to own the whole conversation.

- **Inbound calls**: It answers, works out what the caller needs and takes their details, so a busy front desk doesn't lose the call.
- **Appointment booking**: It checks the open slots in your calendar, offers them on the call and books the one the caller picks.
- **CRM updates**: It creates the lead and the deal, then tells your team a new lead has landed. Nobody retypes anything.
- **Outbound calls**: It dials a list you supply, holds the conversation and makes the pitch.

That is roughly $0.09 to $0.14 a minute, so 1,000 minutes of calls a month runs about $90 to $140.

## WhatsApp AI chatbots
A WhatsApp AI chatbot reads what customers write, asks only for what is missing and hands your team a clean, confirmed request. The one I built runs in production for a travel agency, in Roman Urdu, Urdu, English and Arabic, and nothing reaches the team until the customer has confirmed it.

- **Real, messy messages**: Customers leave out dates, correct themselves and ask about several cities in one thread. The bot keeps what they said and asks only for what is missing.
- **Your customers' languages**: It replies in the language the customer wrote in, Roman Urdu included, and ignores the greetings and chatter that would otherwise open false leads.
- **A person stays in charge**: Every confirmed lead gets an ID and goes to your team's WhatsApp group. A team member answers by swiping on it, and the answer reaches the right customer.
- **The daily work around it**: A daily operations summary goes to the team's group, and an invoice swiped in the group is forwarded to the right customer.

Meta changed this on 1 October 2026: replies beyond the free tier are now charged, so cost guides from before that date will say they are free.
